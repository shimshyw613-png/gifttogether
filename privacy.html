What GiftTogether is

GiftTogether helps families, communities, classes and groups of friends organize group gifts, birthdays and family dates in private groups.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in.
  • What you add: your name, birthday, phone, home address, the groups you join, celebrations, the amounts you choose to chip in, card messages, wishes and dates.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your phone’s notification address.

We do not collect card or bank details. Payments happen outside the app, for example by Zelle or cash.

Who can see it

  • Only members of a group can see that group. Groups can’t see each other.
  • How much you give is visible only to you and the celebration’s organizer.
  • A surprise celebration is hidden from the people it’s hidden from until the organizer reveals it.
  • You can hide your phone, email and address from the group. Group admins can still see them so gifts can reach you.

How we use it

Only to run GiftTogether: signing you in, showing your groups, and sending the emails and notifications you expect. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services we use

Supabase stores the data. [Email provider, for example Brevo] sends sign-in emails. Apple and Google deliver phone notifications.

Your choices

You can edit your details in My info, leave any group, or ask us to delete your account and information by writing to [contact email]. We will do so within 30 days.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Parents and group admins may add children’s names and birthdays to a group without giving them an account.
